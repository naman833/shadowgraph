# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ShadowGraph community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or discriminatory language
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, insults, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without permission
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through
the contact channel listed in the repository's hosting settings. Maintainers
will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may
remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban participants.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.
